What is Noah?

Noah combines voice interaction, artificial intelligence, memory, sensors and robotic control into one student-focused system.

Learning

Helps students understand concepts and work through learning problems.

Skills

Helps students discover and develop useful skills beyond academic marks.

Future Planning

Helps students explore interests, goals and possible future paths.

AI Interaction

Uses voice recognition, artificial intelligence and speech output to communicate with users.

Noah Guide Book

Basic information for installing, operating and understanding Noah.

Noah is an AI-powered student-guidance robot. Its purpose is to help students learn, develop skills, explore interests and plan goals.
The current robot design uses a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W as the main computer.
Noah can use a microphone for speech input, speech recognition to understand the user, an AI brain for responses and a speaker for voice output.
